Wealth Trajectories: Adjusted National Income per Capita, 2000 vs. 2021

Adjusted net national income strips out capital depreciation and includes net foreign transfers — a more sustainable measure of wealth than GDP. Denmark more than doubled; Azerbaijan quadrupled; Burundi and DR Congo remain under $400.

Source: World Bank — Adjusted Net National Income per Capita (NY.ADJ.NNTY.PC.CD)
