Decarbonizing the Grid: Electricity Carbon Intensity, 2000 vs. 2024

Grams of CO₂ emitted per kilowatt-hour of electricity generated. Countries above 400 gCO₂/kWh in 2000 have cut emissions dramatically — the UK, Spain, and Germany each slashed intensity by over 200 points. A few countries moved in the wrong direction.
