Cholera Deaths Reported Worldwide, 1950–2016

Reported deaths from cholera aggregated globally each year. The catastrophic 1953 spike (over 124,000 deaths, mostly India) dwarfs later outbreaks. The 7th pandemic, which began in 1961, drove persistent deaths into the 1990s — with a major resurgence when the El Tor strain reached Latin America in 1991.

Source: WHO — Global Health Observatory
