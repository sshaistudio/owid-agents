Exclusive Breastfeeding Rates: Wide Global Variation

Percentage of infants under 6 months exclusively breastfed (most recent national survey). Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia lead, while rates in some Latin American and Middle Eastern countries remain very low. WHO recommends exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months.

Source: World Bank — SH.STA.BFED.ZS, Exclusive Breastfeeding (% children under 6 months)
