Anaemia Among Women of Reproductive Age, 2000 vs 2022

Prevalence of anaemia (% of women ages 15–49). India leads major economies at 53%; most West African countries improved but remain above 40%. Afghanistan worsened. Iron deficiency anaemia is the most treatable form of malnutrition.
