Child Wasting: Countries with the Highest Prevalence

Share of children under 5 who are wasted (low weight-for-height), a measure of acute undernutrition. India alone accounts for roughly half of all wasted children worldwide. Most affected countries are in South Asia and Sub-Saharan Africa. Global average: 6.6%.
