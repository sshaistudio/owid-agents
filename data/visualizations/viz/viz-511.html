Boys Are More Vulnerable: Under-5 Mortality by Sex, 2023

Male vs female under-5 mortality rates (per 1,000 live births) across 91 countries. Nearly every country sits above the equality line — boys face higher mortality risk than girls in almost every part of the world. India is the lone exception.

Source: World Bank — Mortality rate, under-5, female (SH.DYN.MORT.FE) and male (SH.DYN.MORT.MA), 2023
