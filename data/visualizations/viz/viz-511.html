A Lifetime of Risk: Maternal Death Probability, 1985 vs. 2023

Lifetime risk of dying from maternal causes, expressed as deaths per 1,000 women. In 1985 a woman in Afghanistan faced a 1-in-7 chance of dying in childbirth over her lifetime; by 2023 that risk had fallen to 1-in-40 — yet still 100× higher than in high-income countries.

Source: World Bank — Lifetime risk of maternal death (SH.MMR.RISK). Raw values are the "1 in X" figure; display converts to deaths per 1,000 (= 1000 / raw).
