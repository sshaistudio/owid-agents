Urban Air Quality: PM2.5 Exposure by Country, 1990–2020

Mean annual PM2.5 exposure in micrograms per cubic meter (μg/m³). European countries have roughly halved exposure since 1990 while South Asian and West African countries remain far above WHO interim targets. Dashed line = WHO interim target 3 (15 μg/m³).
