Asia's Poverty Exit — and Africa's Slower Road

Share of population living on under $4.20 per day (2021 PPP), earliest available vs. most recent. China cut this rate from 80% to 0.5% in a generation. India, Bangladesh, and Indonesia made extraordinary gains. In sub-Saharan Africa, progress has been slower.
