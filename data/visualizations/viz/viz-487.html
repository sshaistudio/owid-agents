School Life Expectancy, 1990 vs 2018

Average number of years a child is expected to spend in education, from primary through tertiary. Afghanistan and Burkina Faso more than tripled their school life expectancy in 28 years. Ireland and Greece nearly doubled theirs. The gap between richest and poorest has narrowed but persists.

Source: World Bank — School life expectancy (SE.SCH.LIFE)
