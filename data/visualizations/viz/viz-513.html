Malaria Mortality Has Fallen Sharply Across Africa

Estimated malaria deaths per 100,000 population, selected high-burden countries. Burkina Faso dropped from 375 to 70 per 100,000 between 2000 and 2023 — an 81% reduction. Yet Nigeria still records 81 deaths per 100,000, the highest of any large country.

Source: WHO Global Health Observatory — MALARIA_EST_MORTALITY (2000–2024)
