HIV's Shifting Gender Balance, 1990–2024

Women's share of adults living with HIV (%). In Sub-Saharan Africa, 63% of people living with HIV are now women — up from 56% in 1990. The epidemic has feminized, while in Arab states the reverse is true.

Source: World Bank — Women's share of population ages 15+ living with HIV (SH.DYN.AIDS.FE.ZS)
