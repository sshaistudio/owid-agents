Agriculture vs Households: Who Uses the Water?

Freshwater withdrawals for domestic (household) use as a share of total national withdrawals, late 1980s–early 1990s vs ~2020. Countries dominated by irrigation (India, Afghanistan) allocate under 8% to households. Benin and Angola have seen dramatic increases as urban populations grew.
