Shrinking Farmland: Arable Land per Person, 1961–2023

Hectares of arable land available per person. The global average has halved since 1961 as population growth outpaces land conversion. Egypt and Bangladesh now have under 0.05 ha per person — roughly half a tennis court.

Source: World Bank — Arable Land (Hectares per Person) (AG.LND.ARBL.HA.PC)
