Energy Use per Capita: 1990 vs 2020

Kilograms of oil equivalent per person. Iceland more than doubled its already-high consumption. South Korea and China tripled theirs. Most Western economies declined slightly. India and Bangladesh started very low and grew modestly, consuming 15–25× less than the highest users.

Source: World Bank — EG.USE.PCAP.KG.OE (Energy Use per Capita, kg of Oil Equivalent)
