Who Gets the Smallest Slice: Income Share of the Poorest 20%

Share of national income held by the bottom quintile (~2000 vs ~2020). Colombia, Brazil, and Honduras remain among the world's most unequal. Kazakhstan tripled the bottom quintile's share. India's bottom 20% receive over 10% — more than most Latin American countries.
