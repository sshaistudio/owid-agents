Youth Dependency Ratio: 2000 vs 2023

Children (0-14) per 100 working-age people (15-64). Lines show change over 23 years, sorted by 2023 value.

Central African Republic (101%) and Chad (91%) carry the world's heaviest youth burden -- more children than workers. Korea (15.5%) and Japan (19.8%) face the opposite challenge. Afghanistan fell sharply (105% → 80%) as fertility declined.

Source: World Bank, SP.POP.DPND.YG
