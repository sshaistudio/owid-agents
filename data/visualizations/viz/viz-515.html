Women's Share of People Living with HIV, 1990–2023

HIV in Sub-Saharan Africa is a woman's disease. Nearly 63% of people living with HIV in the region are women — a share still rising. Globally, women account for 53% and rising, while the epidemic in other regions skews male.

Source: World Bank — Women's Share of Population Ages 15+ Living with HIV (%) (SH.DYN.AIDS.FE.ZS)
