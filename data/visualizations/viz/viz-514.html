The Sanitation Leap: Safely Managed Sanitation, ~2000 vs 2024

Share of population using safely managed sanitation services (%). India's jump from 5% to 64% stands out as one of the largest improvements in a generation. Kuwait and Singapore held steady at 100%, while DR Congo and Yemen slid backward.
