The Mental Health Workforce Gap: Psychiatrists per 100,000 People

Norway has 48 psychiatrists per 100,000 people. Chad has 0.007. This gap — more than 6,000-fold — reflects the extreme concentration of mental health capacity in high-income countries, with most of Africa operating with near-zero specialist coverage.

Source: WHO Global Health Observatory — Psychiatrists working in mental health sector (MH_6), ~2013–2017
