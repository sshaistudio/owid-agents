Lifetime Risk of Maternal Death: 1990 vs 2022

In Afghanistan, a woman once faced a 1-in-9 lifetime chance of dying in childbirth. By 2022 that had fallen to 1-in-37. Most high-burden countries saw dramatic reductions — but Chad's risk barely halved.

Source: World Bank — Lifetime Risk of Maternal Death (%) (SH.MMR.RISK.ZS)
