Deaths from Non-Communicable Diseases: 2000 vs. 2019

Share of deaths from NCDs (heart disease, cancer, diabetes, stroke). The global epidemiological transition is visible: nearly every country moved toward a higher NCD share as infectious disease mortality fell — but the gap between rich and poor nations remains vast.
