HIV New Infections in Sub-Saharan Africa, 1990–2024

New HIV infections per 1,000 uninfected people ages 15–49. The crisis peaked in the early 1990s; sustained treatment and prevention efforts have cut incidence by over 85% in most countries, though Eswatini and Botswana remain far above the global average.
