Raised Fasting Blood Glucose Prevalence, ~2006

Share of adults with fasting blood glucose ≥ 7.0 mmol/L (age-standardized %). Values above 7% indicate high-risk diabetes prevalence. Pacific island states and Middle Eastern countries lead globally.

Source: WHO Global Health Observatory — Raised Fasting Blood Glucose, Age-Standardized (NCD_GLUC_04)
