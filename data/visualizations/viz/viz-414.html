Energy Use per Capita, 1990-2020

Kilograms of oil equivalent per person per year. Sorted from highest to lowest energy consumption.

Iceland (16,084 kgoe) uses 57x more energy per person than Bangladesh (279 kgoe). High-income countries have plateaued or declined. China tripled its use. Ethiopia barely moved despite growth.
