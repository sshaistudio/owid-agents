Severe Wasting in Children Under 5: A Slow Decline

Severe wasting prevalence (weight-for-height below −3 SD) based on national surveys. Mali peaked at 12.9% in the 1990s; India, Yemen, and Chad remain high. Bangladesh has made the most sustained progress, from 7% down to under 2%.
