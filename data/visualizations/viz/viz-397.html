The Surgery Gap: Financial Risk of Surgical Care by Country

Share of population at risk of catastrophic out-of-pocket expenditure if they need surgical care (%, most recent year 2019–2022). In Côte d'Ivoire and Burundi, virtually the entire population faces this risk. In high-income countries with universal coverage, it approaches zero.
