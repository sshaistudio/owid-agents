Armed Forces as a Share of the Labor Force: 1990 vs. 2020

Military personnel as a percentage of the total labor force. The end of the Cold War and Gulf War demobilization drove large declines in Iraq, Israel, and Jordan. North Korea remains an outlier. Most high-income countries converged toward minimal military burden.
