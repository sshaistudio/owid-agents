Childhood Leukaemia: 5-Year Survival Rate by Country, 2021

Age-standardized 5-year net survival (%) for children diagnosed with lymphoid leukaemia. Finland achieves 93% survival; children in C. African Republic face just 19% — a 74-point gap driven by chemotherapy access and healthcare capacity.
