Anemia Among Women of Reproductive Age, 2000–2023

Percentage of women ages 15–49 with anemia in the 30 highest-burden countries. The trend varies: many West African countries have declined but remain above 40%; India has barely moved; Afghanistan and Bangladesh are rising. Each sparkline covers 2000–2023.

Source: World Bank — Prevalence of Anemia, Women of Reproductive Age (SH.ANM.ALLW.ZS)
