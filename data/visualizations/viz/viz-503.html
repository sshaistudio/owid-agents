Tuberculosis Treatment Success: Progress With a Paradox

% of new TB cases successfully treated: 2005 vs. 2022. Most high-burden developing countries have improved dramatically. Paradoxically, some European countries have seen success rates plummet—likely due to treating increasingly complex MDR cases and loss-to-follow-up in low-incidence settings.

Source: World Bank — Tuberculosis Treatment Success Rate (SH.TBS.CURE.ZS) · WHO
