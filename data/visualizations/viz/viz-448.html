The Gender Labor Gap: From Arab World to North America

Female labor force participation as a % of male rate, by world region, 1990–2024. 100 = full parity. The Arab World remains below 31%; Latin America has converged from 53% to 69%; Sub-Saharan Africa has always been near-equal.

Source: World Bank — Ratio of Female to Male Labor Force Participation Rate (ILO estimate)
