Patent Applications by Residents, 1990–2021

Number of patent applications filed by residents in each country's patent office. China's surge — from 5,800 applications in 1990 to 1.4 million in 2021 — is without historical parallel. Japan and Germany have plateaued or declined; South Korea and India are rising steadily.

Source: World Bank — Patent Applications, Residents (IP.PAT.RESD)
