Energy Intensity of the Economy, 1990 vs 2022

Kg of oil equivalent per $1,000 GDP (PPP). Post-Soviet economies like Bosnia, Estonia, and Armenia started the 1990s extraordinarily energy-hungry; Ireland and Denmark show that rich economies can get far more output per unit of energy.
